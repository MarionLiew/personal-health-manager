# Lifestyle guidance

Recommendations describe the observed trend, time range, completeness, concrete behavior, duration,
review date, success metric, uncertainty and clinician-stop conditions. At most three priority
actions are returned. Weight uses rolling/trend statistics instead of daily noise; sleep prioritizes
duration and regularity; HRV/resting heart rate are compared only with personal baselines.

Wearables, sleep stages, a single HRV day, and consumer body composition do not diagnose infection,
inflammation, overtraining or cancer. Associations are not described as causation. Prescription
drugs or high-dose supplements are outside scope.
